agent-ops privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

agent-ops is a personal tool of Carlos Corrêa, used only by him with his own Google accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: the events of the calendars he owns, to show them in one place and to keep his own calendars in step.
  • Gmail: sending email he approves, from his own account, and reading his own messages when he asks.
  • His account's email address, to confirm which of his accounts signed in.

How the data is kept

The data is processed and stored on his own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. The data is used only to provide these features to him; it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's settings, under third-party connections (myaccount.google.com/connections).
